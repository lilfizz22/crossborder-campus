# CrossBorder Campus - AI Life Assistant for International Students

CrossBorder Campus is an AI life assistant for international students living between two cities, like Singapore and China.

## Problem
International students juggle two currencies, two timelines, visa deadlines, and budgets in SGD and CNY. Exchange rates move daily, and important dates like return flights, visa expiry, and scholarship deadlines are easy to miss.

## Solution
A boarding-pass style app that shows your Singapore to China to Singapore journey, live SGD to CNY rates, budget tracking, and AI budget advice in one place.

## Features
- Dual-city timeline with countdowns
- Live FX converter with DBS Remit cashback calculation
- Expense tracking in SGD and CNY
- AI Budget Coach that gives structured saving tips from your anonymised totals
- Reminders and checklists for return and re-entry
- Demo Mode that switches all data to fictional demo data for privacy

## AI Implementation
- AI Budget Coach uses a large language model to analyse anonymised monthly totals and give personalised saving advice
- Live FX data from ExchangeRate-API
- Rule-based reminders for deadlines
